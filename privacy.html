Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026. Developer: Udbhav Naryani.

Summary

Ekant has no account, no backend, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We collect no data.

Data on your device

Your practice data is stored only on your device (SwiftData). A small snapshot is shared through an App Group solely so the Home Screen widget can show today's streak. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Microphone

The microphone is used only if you turn on the optional “voice counting” feature (not available in the current public release). Audio is processed in memory on your device to count repetitions. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Premium (annual subscription with a 7-day free trial, and lifetime) is handled by Apple. The developer receives no payment details.

Export and deletion

Settings lets you export your data and delete all of it. Both are always free.

Not medical advice

Ekant makes no medical or health claims.
