Ekant

A calm daily Hindu mantra and japa practice. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Your data

Your practice stays on your device. In Settings you can export your data or delete all of it at any time, and both are always free.

Home Screen widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for “Ekant” and choose a size. The widget shows today's streak.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications set on your device. You can change or turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings.

Premium

Premium is available as an annual subscription with a 7-day free trial, or as a lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple; manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings, and use Restore Purchases in the app if needed.
